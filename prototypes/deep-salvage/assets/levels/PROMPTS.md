# Level-card artwork

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-27. These three WebP files are the level-picker backgrounds. Original generations were 1536 × 1024 PNGs; deployment copies retain the composition at 768 × 512, encoded as WebP at quality 84. No extra image-generation dependency is required to run the game.

## Saved assets

- `sunken-city.webp`
- `kelp-wilds.webp`
- `cinder-foundry.webp`

## Final prompts

### city

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape background illustration for a level-selection card in Deep Salvage, a cartoon submarine engineering mobile game. Wide 3:2 landscape image, composed to remain legible when cropped into a wide card. Style: charming hand-painted 2D game environment, bold dark teal outlines, simplified rounded silhouettes, rich layered depth, soft underwater light, slightly dystopian but playful, matching a little cartoon submarine game. Environment only: no submarine, no characters, no enemies, no UI, no letters, no text, no border, no watermark. Keep the lower-left quarter darker and visually quiet so the app can overlay a white level name; distinctive landmarks across the center and right. Sunken City: drowned city streets and crooked watchtowers under turquoise blue water, broken elevated walkways, submerged apartment towers receding into mist, small coral and seaweed at the bases, gentle sunbeams from above. Cool teal and blue palette with tiny warm windows.
