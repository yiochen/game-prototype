# Checkpoint expedition gameplay and controls

The user accepted [the consolidated expedition model](../expedition-gameplay-and-controls.md) on 2026-10-05: automatic forward travel with drag steering, a final harpoon-and-follow creature encounter that unlocks a recipe, and passive night recharging between expeditions. Preserve this baseline and its separate salvage economy while prototyping; numeric balance, encounter content, and presentation remain open rather than becoming additional assumed rules.
