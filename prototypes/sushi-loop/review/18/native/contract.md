# Issue 18 · approved Shop carried into native Godot

The user approved the complete interactive Shop after revising it to Edit-only inventory-tray entry, no left HUD Shop, hidden inventory while browsing, the existing round X, maple popup and rounded beige scroll, bag tap returning to inventory, and a slower 1.1-second item flight. The accepted reference is https://6ae16dc4693b906640736dcd--game-prototypes-yiochen.netlify.app/prototypes/sushi-loop/design/shop-preview/ . That approval authorized native integration and a release APK upload to Google Drive.

`shop_presentation.gd` supplies gameplay and `--shop-gallery`. `maple_popup_skin.gd` shares the recipe picker's exact maple shader setup and close styling. Existing Blender goods, grain and icons supply the family; the bag is the editable SVG from the approved prototype's geometry. The catalog mixes unlimited belt tiles/stools and finite plant/crate/barrel goods without section headings. Native dynamic labels retain item-specific accessible Buy names and costs while disabled/sold out. Rounded clipping belongs to the beige catalog; flights live in their own unclipped overlay layer. The bag and X remain usable while paid items travel. Touch, keyboard, system Back and canceled gestures preserve state. No new functional furniture benefits, stock rotation or floor unlocks are invented here.

`shop_book.gd` and `content/shop.json` keep rules and tuning separate. A purchase requires Edit, a valid offer, stock, affordability and the current purchase sequence. It debits once, adds to existing inventory, decreases finite stock and advances the replay token. All fields serialize together through SaveStore before animation. Existing editor conservation checks include purchased lifetime quantities, so placed/stored goods remain valid after reload. Old saves add zero-purchase Shop metadata once, without changing coins, inventory, camera, progression, clocks or RNG.

Failed writes preserve the accepted purchase in memory and block further spending through the existing recovery panel. Retry saves that exact purchase and only then resumes presentation. Closing, backgrounding or reduced motion do not affect payment. The whole editor is hidden while Shop is open and returns with the same category, scroll, camera and opener focus.

Native UI uses the gameplay portrait's uniform scale, including high-resolution Android-sized windows; it never applies a desktop physical-pixel cap to a dense phone. Approved grain tiles rather than stretching a whole surface, rounded corners retain their proportions, and button art is separate from stable native hit targets. The original game world, sprite registration and projection are unchanged.
