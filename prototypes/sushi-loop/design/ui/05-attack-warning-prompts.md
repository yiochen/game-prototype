Preserve portrait dimensions, finished 2D doodle ink style, navy/teal scenery, all reef/wreck/pickup placement, eel size and position, top hull badge, salvage "72" badge, pause, exact "SPOTTED EEL" name and resistance bar. Keep the mint following strip with two dashed vertical edges at approximately 34% and 66% screen width, extending uninterrupted top to bottom behind the opaque HUD, without vertical ends.

Its whole hull should fit inside the mint following strip and remain LEFT of the imminent attack warning.
```text
The eel is winding up an electrical attack, with just two or three tiny pale electric sparks around its head. No active beam or discharge, no damage, no floating text, no new HUD, no timers, no arrows or harpoon buttons.
Mark one selected narrow vertical ATTACK STRIP between 56% and 65% screen width, extending from screen top to bottom behind the opaque HUD. This overlaps the RIGHT part of the mint following strip, leaving safe water on the left for the ship. The warning must be visually distinct from the safe mint region. A small simple lightning-bolt warning icon appears inside the attack strip at about 21% screen height. Exact strip widths are visual examples, not fixed gameplay constants.
Option A: attack warning uses translucent AMBER fill with widely spaced AMBER diagonal hazard hatching across its full height, and two restrained amber side outlines. Seabed stays visible, while hatching clearly says danger. Keep diagonal stripes ONLY inside the narrow attack band, never in the rest of the following region. The lightning icon is amber/ivory with ink outline. Avoid opaque yellow wall, whole-screen tint, active electricity beam, or additional words.
```
