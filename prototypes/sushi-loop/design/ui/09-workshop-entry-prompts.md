# Workshop restaurant-entry options

Built-in image generation variations based on the restaurant half of [the selected doodle art reference](../art-directions/01-doodle.png). Pending UI interview Question 9. Common HUD/toolbar arrangement is illustrative; this question concerns only Workshop access. Restaurant cell-occupancy rules remain authoritative.

## A — fixed toolbar entry

```text
Use case: ui-mockup, polished commercial 2D mobile idle-game UI.
Image 1 is a style reference and starting art target. Use ONLY the RESTAURANT on its LEFT, expand/recompose it into one full PORTRAIT 9:16 mobile game screenshot. Do not include the underwater panel or a split-screen. Preserve the selected doodle visual language: confident slightly irregular ink outlines, warm ivory square floor, coral conveyor trim, mustard and mint accents, modular expressive 2D sprite-like people, flat colors and restrained grain. No painterly concept art, no 3D, no isometric diamond tiles, no phone frame.
Show the LIVE restaurant view with a clear orthogonal SQUARE CELL GRID, eight columns and ten rows visible. Every belt tile, chef and occupied customer seat occupies exactly ONE square cell. Cell size visually consistent, grid aligned, subtle floor grout under empty cells. Make people and sushi readable at phone size, commercially polished artwork and clean UI.
A cleared rectangular workable area in the center is bordered by garbage piles, cardboard and cracked tiles around the outer edges. Floor is warm ivory; outer garbage muted so activity dominates. Leave clear aisles for walking customers.
Conveyor is an OPEN U-shaped nine-tile belt: left vertical arm, three-tile bottom horizontal turn, right vertical arm. No junctions, no full loop. Dishes on individual belt cells, one plate per cell, arrows on belt indicate motion. Do not pile dishes or drop them on floor.
Two chefs each stand in an ordinary floor cell BESIDE a belt tile, never on the conveyor or on an endpoint. Each chef's tiny dish blackboard is integrated WITHIN that chef's own cell footprint, not a freestanding extra-cell sign. Three occupied customer seats each face one adjacent belt cell; each seated person and seat fit together in one cell. Customers have different expressions; one has a small sushi thought bubble with a circular preference countdown, another is happily eating. Two walking customers in separate aisle cells.
Top overlay HUD: ivory coin badge with exact balance "1,240" at upper left; a compact small submarine battery icon partly filled at upper right, no countdown text. No earnings rate, reports, customer fullness bars or plate-count targets.
Bottom LIVE-view toolbar has matching ivory rounded ink-outlined buttons, a hammer icon labeled "Edit", chef face labeled "Staff", recipe book labeled "Recipes". These are GLOBAL live-view buttons, NOT the icon-only edit-layer controls. Actual edit mode is not shown. No build tray, selection outlines or rotate controls.
Keep the restaurant scene large, bright and readable, with deliberate spacing, friendly animated-sprite appearance, and polished consistent icon style. Exact common toolbar/HUD layout is illustrative; the compared decision is only Workshop access.
OPTION A — fixed Workshop entry: add a FOURTH bottom toolbar button with a clear wrench icon and exact label "Workshop". All four buttons remain fixed to the screen, evenly spaced in one small bottom row. No Workshop doorway or sign in the scene. Upper scene wall has the normal restaurant entrance and simple lanterns only.
```

## Targeted correction applied to both variants

The initial renders introduced numbered preference bubbles and separate chef blackboards. A shared edit removes those inconsistencies and reduces sprite footprints. Only corrected outputs are saved as final options.

```text
Precise correction of Image 1. Keep its entire restaurant scene, camera, doodle style, floor grid, conveyor route, plates, coin HUD, battery HUD, lanterns, entrance, garbage and ALL navigation controls/labels unchanged. Preserve the existing Workshop access method exactly as pictured.
Correct ONLY the character presentation:
1. Remove the number "3" and its tiny numbered circle from every sushi preference thought bubble. Instead give the sushi thought bubble a simple partial circular orange countdown ring around its outside edge, with NO numerals. The player never sees a customer's target dish count or fullness.
2. Resize each chef, seated customer plus seat, and walking customer into a compact full-body 2D game sprite that fits one existing square floor cell. Use the visible grout spacing as the cell size, keep foot positions aligned and expressive faces. Each chef stands beside the conveyor, not on it. Customer seats face their adjacent conveyor tile. Keep characters large enough to read, but not spanning multiple floor cells. No new people.
3. Remove all freestanding recipe blackboards. Each chef instead has one tiny black recipe board mounted within their own cell, just in front of their apron/feet, showing the dish they are making. It must overlap the chef's own footprint rather than sit in a neighboring cell. No extra furniture cell or separate board stand.
Maintain polished 2D sprite and ink rendering, not wireframe or prototype. Do not alter labels, create new buttons, or change the Workshop entry.
```
