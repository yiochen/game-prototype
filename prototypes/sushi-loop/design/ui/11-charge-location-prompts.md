# Restaurant charge-indicator location options

Pending UI interview Question 11. Option A is a built-in image generation edit of [the accepted docked-submarine reference](./10b-expedition-dock.png). Option B reuses that reference's persistent top-right charge badge. These compare location only; charge timing and readiness rules remain unchanged.

## A — dock-local battery indicator

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit, polished commercial 2D mobile-game UI.
Image 1 is the selected docked-submarine restaurant scene. Preserve EVERYTHING about the scene and UI: portrait dimensions, commercial doodle ink style, warm square-cell floor, coral open conveyor and dishes, chefs with tiny blackboards in their own footprints, seated and walking customers, unnumbered circular sushi preference timer, Workshop doorway with exact "Workshop" name, actual restaurant entrance, yellow submarine and water/jetty at upper left with exact "Expedition" sign, coin balance "1,240", and bottom Edit/Staff/Recipes buttons.
Change ONLY charge-indicator placement:
1. REMOVE the entire floating top-right submarine/battery HUD badge. Restore the wall or scenery behind it, matching existing wood and stone. Keep the Workshop doorway below unchanged.
2. Add ONE small readable physical battery-status placard attached to the dock railing, immediately above the Expedition sign or beside the docked yellow submarine. It belongs to the SCENE, not the screen HUD: a tiny ivory/wood plate with ink border and a simple horizontal green battery icon filled about two-thirds, with the remaining third unfilled. Its apparent size should be about 15–18% of the screen width, readable without covering the submarine, cash badge or Expedition lettering. Keep it compact and integrated with the dock.
No additional top charge badge, no numbers, countdown, percentage, Ready label, arrow, launch button, new currency or new toolbar entry. This comparison changes only where the battery status lives; the dock remains the expedition entrance. Do not alter characters, layout, scenery scale, icon style, or add clutter.
```
