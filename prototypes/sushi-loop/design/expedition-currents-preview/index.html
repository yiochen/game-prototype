Rules in this prototype

  • Currents push sideways at columns/s, on top of steering (). Letting go eases your steering to zero; the push continues.
  • Shield: × forward speed for s and no collision damage. No pickup multiplier. It ends at the encounter edge (the finish chunk stands in for the creature encounter: no shields or portals there).
