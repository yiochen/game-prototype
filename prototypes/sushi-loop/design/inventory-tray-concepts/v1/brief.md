The complete composition preserves the real gameplay scene, existing upper HUD, indigo category curtains, Shop position and compact bottom-dock footprint. The redesign makes the merchandise display a coordinated ceramic inset in a cedar drawer, replaces paging carets with a horizontal strip, and shows a clipped next item.
