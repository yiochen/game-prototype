Cash notes & tip jar · issue #61 art review

Floor cash

Tap a pile to collect it, plus any pile within one tile. Piles sort with the scene, so furniture and guests in front hide them, but taps still reach a hidden pile (try the one behind the guest at the lower belt).

Tip jar

Placed at the lower left. When no tile around the seat is free, the tip flies into the jar (only what fits). Tap the jar to collect its balance.
