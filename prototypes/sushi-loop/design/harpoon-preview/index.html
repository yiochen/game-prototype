Try it

Steer by holding ←/→ or either half of the playfield. Fire with the Harpoon button or Space. Each fresh press fires once; holding never repeats. A miss reels back in and the ring fills before the next shot.

The shooting window has no countdown. Shortly before it closes the eel turns restless (twitchier, flickering), and then it escapes.
