Just-in-time callouts

These replace the explanation text on screens. A callout is one short line, anchored to the room, button or bubble it's about. Only one shows at a time, and it fades after about 3 seconds or on any tap. It never blocks input. "Once" callouts are saved, so they never repeat; problem callouts repeat at most once a minute for each room.

Decisions this IA makes (for review)

1Tapping a room selects it. It doesn't enter.The design doc says "tapping a room enters". A sheet first gives the grid somewhere for pipes, upgrades and demolish. Tapping again, double-tapping or the Enter button goes in. Quick actions sit in bubbles, so collecting never needs the sheet.
2Pipes are edited only on the grid.Rooms show their inlets and outlets but don't edit links. A connector tapped inside a room jumps to the grid in link mode for that outlet.
3Clearing trash happens in the plot's sheet.One button per piece, with its price. At 52 px a plot is too small to tap the pieces themselves.
4Demolish sits behind ⋯ in the sheet and asks to confirm, showing the refund.It never appears inside the room.
5Building happens in the room, not in a global shop.Each room has a bottom tray of its own pieces (guest room: seats, decor, belt; kitchen: belt, machines, Plater). You pay as you place. Locked pieces show what unlocks them.
6Achievements are a full screen with claimable rewards.🏆 stays on the grid HUD with a red count when something is ready. Reputation and mastery level-ups are achievements, so their coins are claimed instead of paid automatically. 📖 opens the recipe log as its own full screen.
7Portrait only.No landscape layout, including the web prototype.
8Rules are taught by callouts, not labels.Screens show only icons, numbers, bars and buttons. Rooms have no names: a pipe's target is shown on a tiny pier map and by highlighting the pipe. Each rule is explained once, at the moment it first matters (see the callouts table). The away credit becomes a toast instead of a dialog.
