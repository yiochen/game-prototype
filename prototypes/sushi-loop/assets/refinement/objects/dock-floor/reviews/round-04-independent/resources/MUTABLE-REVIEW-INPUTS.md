# Immutable review inputs

Before hashing evidence, retain exact copies of mutable catalog libraries, consumer modules, renderer/shader files, helpers and external skill instructions inside the current object experiment. Audit/replay against those copies, record original paths and hashes as provenance, and bind the retained copy paths in the packet and supplemental evidence manifest. Current global source files can legitimately change during the remaining catalog rollout.

A baseline diagnostic stays bound to its original source/code. New capture or consumer settings require a versioned contract and new evidence. Preserve failed rounds, raw native frames and earlier signed reviews. An exact manifest is useful only when its actual bytes and dependency closure are retained in Git.

Use the actual published actor sprite and matching approved native body for current consumer/physical contexts. Record authored camera differences rather than replacing the real consumer sprite with an unannounced derived projection. Historical bodies and controlled same-camera renders are useful separately labeled diagnostics.
