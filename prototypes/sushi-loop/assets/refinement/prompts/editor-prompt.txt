EDITABLE 3D
Work in an isolated saved project copy. Prefer background Blender where practical; inspect available tools and actual Blender version/RNA instead of assuming APIs. With live tools, inspect addon/scene status and preserve the active document/temporary inspection settings. Do not alter original textures or linked resources. Save changed resources inside the experiment and keep the edited copy self-contained where appropriate.

Possible techniques: real bevels/thickness, continuous topology/clean joins, deliberate curvature/folds, correct face/vertex normals, selective smooth/flat shading or weighted normals, shader bevel, UV/material variation, procedural/painted textures, roughness/specular/coat, controlled AO, broad lights/reflections and appropriate color management. These are options, not required effects. Diagnose geometry/normal faults before hiding them with light. A shader does not repair a silhouette; a mesh bevel alone does not establish good lighting.

Preserve the actual camera, scale, root, frame, footprint, connectors, rig, UVs, contacts and locked parts specified. Lighting symmetry/no-shadow requirements apply only when declared. Do not substitute a camera-facing target card, paint over final exports or generate AI images as apparent 3D progress. Additional AI texture work requires task authorization.
