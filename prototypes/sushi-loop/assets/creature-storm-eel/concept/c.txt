Character concept sheet for an ORIGINAL boss creature in a cozy mobile submarine game: a giant "Storm Eel".

Image roles: Image 1 is the game's underwater art-direction catalog (style reference only: chunky rounded low-poly-ish 3D cartoon forms, soft broad lighting, saturated but friendly blues/greens/corals, clean readable silhouettes). Image 2 is the gameplay screen concept (scale/camera reference only: a top-down three-quarter view of a 7-column underwater tile tunnel with a small yellow submarine). Image 3 is the CURRENT small eel creature (identity reference: keep its palette family of bright cyan-blue body, a glowing yellow lightning stripe and yellow fins, and its cartoon appeal) - the new design must feel like the same species grown into a big, impressive boss, not a different animal.

Size and role: the boss is about 3 grid columns wide and 5 tall in the game, roughly 6-8 times the length of the submarine. It swims ahead of the submarine, gets harpooned, struggles, fires electric discharges down vertical strips, and finally becomes exhausted and is reeled in. It must read clearly from above at phone size, look exciting and a bit menacing, but remain cute-cartoon (this is a sushi restaurant game - no gore, no realistic horror teeth).

Sheet layout on a plain light grey-blue background, no scene, no UI, no text labels:
- Large hero view from the game camera: top-down three-quarter view looking down at about 35 degrees, the eel swimming toward the top of the image in a graceful S-curve.
- A clean side view (profile) of the full body, straight-ish pose.
- A front view of the head, mouth slightly open.
- A small row of 4 thumbnail poses at the bottom: charging electricity (stripe and fins glowing bright), recoiling when hit, darting away, exhausted/limp with dizzy expression.
Keep proportions and details consistent between all views so a 3D modeler can build and rig it (clear body segments, fin attachment points, tail shape). Design for a rigged 3D model: one continuous tapering body suitable for a bone chain, separate fins and frills that can flap.

Design direction C - "Moray Storm Eel": thick moray-style body with a big round head and wide comical mouth with rounded nub teeth, cheek frills that flare like electric gills, lantern-like glowing spots along its flanks plus the lightning stripe, a ribbon fin along the whole underside and top, and a curled tail tip. Bold, characterful, slightly goofy-menacing.
