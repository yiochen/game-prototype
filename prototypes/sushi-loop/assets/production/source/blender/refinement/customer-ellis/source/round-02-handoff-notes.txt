The actually executed body recipe is build_round02.py. It opens the immutable
original source projects in separate background Blender 5.2.2 processes and saves
round-02.blend and round-02-runtime.blend. The full main/runtime render capture
and audit recipe is capture_audit_round02.py. Each source is packed and editable.

reproduce_round02.py is a path-only adapter around that unchanged executed body
recipe. It reads the copied frozen brief and food map from source/resources and
writes separate source/reproductions and candidates/reproduction-round02 outputs.
Twenty study renders from the portable adapter were compared with the final
native frames; their decoded RGBA pixels matched exactly. This is measured local
reproduction evidence, not a cross-renderer or whole-animation bit-identity claim.

The frame outputs are unedited native RGBA renders. Atlases copy every complete
384x640 frame into the original 6x2 / 2304x1280 arrangement with no fitting, warping
or padding changes. Logical frame 192x320/root 96,248/captureScale 2. The camera is
the actual original source camera, not the stale scale 4 source metadata.

The exact canonical food images are packed. FullUV, uniform aspect and each
frozen alpha >= 128 occupied-width/bottom-rim map are fixed in mesh coordinates.
Original food-root, yaw, render-visibility actions and .6 camera-ray depth remain.
The food plane uses one static BLENDED material/no-shadow flag configuration in
every pose and facing. Original DITHERED flags and matched native comparisons
are retained. Food remains 2D; the actor, clothes, shoes and hair are native 3D.

failed native frames, atlases, clips, contexts and logs in draft-01-hand-weights.
The failure and available recipe reconstruction limits are explicit there.
Round02 preserves each original primitive's intended deformation group. All 576
evaluated hand/sole/seat/mouth-carrier surface witnesses were measured against
source transforms with BVH nearest-surface checks, in addition to the bone audit.

on their final frames; desktop dropped 0 decoder frames, phone maximum 2. Every
required native PNG and the source animation keys remain available for inspection.
