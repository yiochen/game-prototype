Portrait play · Touch controls · Local save
No account or internet connection needed to play.

  1. Download the APK from the shared Drive link to your Android phone.
  2. Open the download. If Android asks, allow installation from the browser or Files app you are using.
  3. Install and open the game. Head to your sushi bar, then tap the submarine when you are ready for a voyage.
