    A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

    Welcome, innkeeper.

    Spend your 100 coins to build the tallest paper hotel you can. Choose one of three cards; the other two are replaced. Every new floor arrives at the open top. Finished floors never change.

    Consecutive rooms of one type form a neighborhood. Every new neighborhood adds one reusable balloon to your Dock. Balloon Call turns matching balloons into new rooms; Copycat copies half your largest neighborhood, up to 8 rooms.

    Neighborhood Streak adds +1, +2, +3… for purchases of the same type. Switching type or buying Mosaic ends it; untyped powers pause it. Guest Parade adds +1, +2, +3… bonus rooms when each typed purchase changes guest type. Each bonus batch is one randomly chosen other type, with equal chances. Repeating a purchased type or buying Mosaic ends it; untyped powers pause it. Only one streak can be active. Both streaks bring moving rainbows and celebrating guests. Type Lock keeps typed offers matching for three shops. During a lock, Room Choice is restricted and Mosaic pauses.

    Choose Room Choice’s type before paying. Mosaic’s arrows read left to right on the card, bottom to top on your hotel. Mosaic gets no Room Pattern or Master Fold extras, but Coupon Book still applies.

    All prices must be affordable before any refund. Powers count only through the floors they create. When you can afford no more cards, choose the free Roof card. Its roof drops onto your hotel to complete your stay.

    CardCoinsEffect

    Keys 1 / 2 / 3 buy cards. Escape closes a dialog. Reveal now completes a delivery without changing its result. Reduced motion reveals immediately. Replay uses the same deliveries and choices.
